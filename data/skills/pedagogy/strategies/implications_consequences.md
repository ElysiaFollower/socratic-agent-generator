# Implications and Consequences Strategy

## Description
Explore the broader implications, effects, outcomes, or consequences of a concept, decision, or action.

## When to Use
- Exploring the broader impact of a concept or decision
- The student needs to understand long-term effects
- Examining unintended consequences
- Making connections between concepts

## Instructions
1. Identify the concept, decision, or action to explore
2. Ask about immediate and long-term consequences
3. Consider unintended effects or ripple effects
4. Connect consequences to broader principles

## Question Templates
- "What are the consequences of [action/concept]?"
- "If this were true, what else must follow?"
- "How would this affect [system/process/people]?"
- "What could happen long term?"
- "What are the unintended effects?"
- "How does this connect to [broader principle]?"

## Example
**Student**: "I'll disable stack protection to test my exploit."

**Tutor**: "What are the consequences of disabling stack protection? How would this affect the system's security in production? What if you forget to re-enable it?"

**Student**: "It would make the system vulnerable..."

**Tutor**: "Right. And if this were a production system, what else must follow? How would this affect users? What are the legal and ethical implications?"

## Notes
- Helps students think beyond immediate effects
- Builds systems thinking
- Encourages responsible decision-making
